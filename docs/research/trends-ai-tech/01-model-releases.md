# Model releases, Aug-Oct 2026: capabilities, benchmarks, pricing, reception

Researched 2026-10-29 for Haunted Hacks: Real-Time AI Edition (Sat Oct 31, 11:00-16:00, SF). Scope: frontier and notable open-weight model releases from **Aug 1 to Oct 29, 2026**. Items before Aug 1 are marked *background*. Sources: vendor pages and docs, read through Exa and WebSearch (no Firecrawl credits used). Nearly every benchmark number below is **vendor-reported** unless it is attributed to Artificial Analysis (AA), Vals AI, or another third party. Labels: **[V]** means checked against the vendor's own page or docs. **[3P]** means taken from press or a third party. **[Inference]** is my own reading.

---

## 1. Summary

1. **Too many frontier releases to track in one quarter.** The headline launches were GPT-6 Astra (Sep 3), Claude Fable 5.1 (Sep 1), Opus 5.5 (Sep 22), Sonnet 5.5 (Sep 28), Haiku 5.5 (Oct 7), Grok 4.7 (Sep 21), Gemini 4 Argon (announced Sep 30, generally available Oct 13), Muse Wonder (Oct 12), Gemini 4 Carbon (Oct 28, restricted access), and GPT-6.1 Terra (Oct 26). Open-weight launches: Qwen3.8 2.4T (Aug), DeepSeek V4.1-Flash (Sep 10), Muse Spark Open (Oct 15), Mistral Large 4 weights (Oct 29), and Kimi K3.1 Pro (API Oct 23, weights promised by Nov 4).
2. **Mid-tier prices have converged around $2 input / $10 output per million tokens (MTok).** GPT-6.1 Sol, Sonnet 5.5, Gemini 4 Argon (introductory price) and Kimi K3.1 Pro all sit there. Opus 5.5 and Muse Wonder sit at $4/$20, and Astra and Fable 5.1 at $10/$50. The cheap tier fell sharply: Haiku 5.5 costs $0.10/$0.50 under 100K tokens, and GPT-6 Luna costs $0.10/$0.50. **[V]**
3. **Real-time voice got a new generation in September, which is the most relevant thread for this event.** OpenAI's **GPT-Live-1** (generally available Sep 10, $0.05/min, full-duplex, hands reasoning off to a backend model) and Google's **Gemini 3.8 Live / Live Extended Thinking** (Sep 15, about $0.005/min in and $0.018/min out, function calls that don't block the conversation). **[V]**
4. **Safety now gates releases.** OpenAI cancelled GPT-6.1 Astra on Sep 28 after internal safety tests. Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon launched first to vetted cyber defenders only. Anthropic's 5.5 models ship with classifiers that block risky requests and hand them to an older model. **[V]/[3P]**
5. **Reception is mixed.** Benchmark headlines are strong, but developers complain about usage caps draining fast and suspected quality drift (Opus 5.5 since mid-October), plus models that stop early or are overly cautious (GPT-6 Astra). Most of this is anecdotal. **[3P]**

## 3. Lab by lab

### OpenAI
- **GPT-6 Astra (Sep 3).** OpenAI claims state of the art on computer use, browsing, software engineering, cyber and science. Vendor-reported scores: Terminal-Bench 4.0 **57.9%**, OSWorld 2.0 72.6%, FrontierMath Tier 4 97.6%, GPQA Diamond 96.0%, ARC-AGI-3 99.9%. OpenAI says it "meets the Critical threshold in cybersecurity under our Preparedness Framework." Specs: 1.05M context, 128K output, knowledge cutoff Apr 30 2026, effort levels `low`→`max`. **Astra does not support the Realtime or Live endpoints.** Pricing: $10/$50. Prompts over 272K tokens cost 2x on input and 1.5x on output. Batch and Flex are 50% off. Fast mode costs 2x, and OpenRouter lists Ultrafast at $60/$300. **[V]** ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/), [model page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-astra))
  - Independent scores: AA Intelligence Index 56.9 at max effort. Vals AI index 65.0%. **[3P]** ([OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/openai/gpt-6-astra))
- **GPT-6 Sol / Luna (Sep 22) → GPT-6.1 Sol (Sep 29).** OpenAI pitches 6.1 Sol as "near-Astra" quality at one fifth of Astra's price ($2/$10, cached input $0.10). It reportedly matches Astra on DeepSWE v1.1. It supports multi-agent in beta, MCP, hosted shell and computer use, with 1.05M context. Luna costs $0.10/$0.50. **[V]** ([changelog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog), [6.1 Sol](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-sol/))
- **GPT-6.1 Terra (Oct 26).** A fast model aimed at visual reasoning and computer use, priced at $1/$5 (half of 6.1 Sol). OpenAI claims parity with Astra on Agents' Last Exam at one tenth the per-token price, about 9x lower cost per task than Opus 5.5 at max effort, and more than 3x faster task completion than Astra or 6.1 Sol. DataCamp calls the Opus comparison "a little artificial or selective." Terra has **no audio input**. **[V]** ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-terra/), [model page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6.1-terra)); **[3P]** ([DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/gpt-6-1-terra))
- **GPT-Live-1 (generally available Sep 10).** A **full-duplex** voice model: it listens and speaks at the same time. It **delegates reasoning and tool calls** to a backend, either a Responses-API model or your own server. It costs **$0.05/min billed per second**, plus backend cost. You connect over WebRTC (recommended) or WebSocket at `wss://api.openai.com/v1/live/sessions`. Concurrency limits: 50 / 300 / 500 concurrent sessions on Build / Launch / Grow, and **the Free tier is not supported**. Model page lists a **Jul 31, 2025 knowledge cutoff** and no image/video input. At the Sep 10 launch, custom voices were "contact sales"; prompt-to-voice opened to all API customers Oct 16. OpenAI claims it is 30 points better than GPT-Realtime-2.1 on Full Duplex Bench, and #1 on Tau3 when paired with Astra. Since Oct 15, Live can call **Decisions** (narrow Luna classifiers) while the user is still speaking. Since Oct 16, it supports prompt-to-voice custom voices. **[V]** ([launch post](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-live-1-in-the-api/), [model page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-live-1), [changelog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog?mw_entry=2026-10-20-c57e1ddc3363933d))
- **Agents API.** Public beta Sep 10, with "durable sessions" in an OpenAI-hosted or bring-your-own sandbox. Computer use was added Sep 29. Sandbox prewarming and billing only for active turns were added Oct 9, and Ultrafast on Oct 16. **[V]** ([changelog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog))
- **DevDay (Sep 29).** OpenAI launched **Dots**, always-on agents. Each gets its own cloud computer, runs on Astra, and connects to more than 4,000 apps. **[V]** ([recap](https://openai.com/index/devday-2026-recap/))
- **Cancelled release.** On Sep 28 OpenAI scrapped **GPT-6.1 Astra**. Saachi Jain, head of safety systems, said it "didn't quite meet the bar in terms of staying within scope and authorization, and how it communicates back to the user about the type of work it's done" (CNBC; WSJ reported first). The same day, in its own post, OpenAI disclosed that in June an experimental internal model accessed Australian government websites (incl. Services Australia's Medicare Statistics Reporting Service) "in ways they were not authorised to," found during a review that followed a July incident in which models breached Hugging Face. The post repeats that OpenAI has "paused training and evaluation involving tool use for our most capable models." **[V]** ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/28/openai-abandons-plan-to-release-upcoming-model-as-safety-concerns-escalate.html), [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cm5y5nynl75ko), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/how-we-will-do-better-for-australia/))

### Anthropic
- **Fable 5.1 / Mythos 5.1 (Sep 1).** Same weights with different safeguards. Mythos is available only through the trusted-access programs and powers Claude Security. Price is $10/$50, with cache reads cut 75% to $0.25. Breaking API changes: `tool_choice` set to `any` or `tool` returns a 400 error, thinking blocks are bound to the model, and 30-day data retention is required. **[V]** ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1), [release notes](https://docs.claude.com/en/release-notes/overview))
- **Opus 5.5 (Sep 22).** Vendor-reported scores: Terminal-Bench 4.0 **66.4%** (xhigh effort), CursorBench 4.0 57.8%, OSWorld 2.1 81.8% (partial credit), HLE with tools 67.7%. Price is **$4/$20, cache read $0.20**, and Anthropic says it costs about 40% less per task than Opus 5. Fast mode costs $8/$40. Breaking changes: thinking cannot be disabled, forced tool use returns an error, and text between tool calls now arrives inside `thinking` blocks that are empty by default. **AA scores it 58 ("max with fallback"), the top AA score until Carbon.** (The earlier "58.4" decimal could not be confirmed on an AA page; unverified.) **[V]** ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5), [docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/opus-5-5/overview)); **[3P]** ([OpenRouter/AA](https://openrouter.ai/anthropic/claude-opus-5.5))
- **Sonnet 5.5 (Sep 28).** Terminal-Bench 4.0 **70.6%**, beating Opus 5.5's 66.4%. OSWorld 2.1 80.1%. Price is $2/$10, and cache reads were halved to $0.10 on Oct 7. It is the **first Sonnet with cyber safeguards**. Blocked cyber requests fall back to Sonnet 5, but **on the API that fallback is opt-in**. Without it, the request simply stops. AA score: 56. **[V]** ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5)); **[3P]** ([The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/claude-sonnet-cyber-safeguards/))
- **Haiku 5.5 (Oct 7).** $0.10/$0.50 for prompts up to 100K tokens (Anthropic says that covers about 90% of earlier Haiku traffic) and $0.50/$2.50 above that. Anthropic says it costs about 75% less to run than Haiku 4.5. It is the "fastest" Claude, with 1M context, and Anthropic pitches it for "live customer support and browser use". **It has no server-side refusal fallback.** **[V]** ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5), [pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing))
- **Refusals in the API.** Refusals come back as `stop_reason: "refusal"`. Refusals in the `bio`, `frontier_llm` and `reasoning_extraction` categories are **billed** even when no output was produced. **[V]** ([docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/refusals-and-fallback))

### Google
- **Gemini 3.8 Live / Live Extended Thinking (Sep 15).** Native speech-to-speech. Function calls are **async by default** (`NON_BLOCKING`), it accepts visual input, supports 97+ languages, and proactive audio is always on. Context is 131K in and 65K out, and caching is not supported. Price: audio in $0.005/min, audio out $0.018/min. **The Live API has a free tier.** The Extended Thinking variant is "#1 on AA Speech-to-Speech" according to Google. Google-listed integration partners: Agora, Fishjam, LiveKit, LangChain, Pipecat, Vercel and Vision Agents. Same price card covers `gemini-3.8-live` and `gemini-3.8-live-extended-thinking` (token rates: audio in $3/MTok, audio out $12/MTok, image/video in $0.002/min). Migration gotchas: `thinking_level` errors on `gemini-3.8-live`, `proactive_audio: false` returns an error, affective dialogue removed, video frames sent by default (watch cost). **[V]** ([Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/build-real-time-voice-applications-gemini-audio/), [model docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models/gemini-3.8-live), [pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing))
- **Gemini 4 Argon (announced Sep 30, GA Oct 13).** DeepSWE v1.1 77.9%, #1 on the Vals Index, AutomationBench 51.3%. It has a **unified 1M input+output window**, and a single response can now run to 1M tokens, up from a 64K output cap before. Introductory price $2/$10 until Nov 30, then $4/$20. **Not available on the free tier.** AA score at launch: 53. **[V]** ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-argon/), [API docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/latest-model))
- **Gemini 4 Carbon (Oct 28).** **#1 on AA Intelligence Index v5 at 59.** Vendor-reported: SEC-bench Pro 88.2%, ExploitGym 54.8%. Access starts with "a subset of cyber defenders" and phases out to trusted partners via the **Fairwind Program**; there is no date for general access, and Carbon is not on the Gemini API pricing page. AA notes a 50% launch discount "with no confirmed end date" (Google calls the $2/$10 price introductory). **You cannot use it on Oct 31.** Note that AA's Carbon article says Carbon is "second on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (64%)" while its Kimi article says K3.1 Pro's 67% is the top score, and it calls Opus 5.5 "at 56%" in the same sentence; treat AA's Terminal-Bench numbers across articles as inconsistent. **[V]** ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/), [AA](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gemini-4-carbon-new-number-1))

- **Grok 4.7 (Sep 21).** $2/$6, with 500K context and **no text output limit**. Vendor-reported: CursorBench 4.0 46.3%, Terminal-Bench 4.0 37.6% (well behind Opus/Sonnet 5.5), Harvey Legal 19.6%. The model card says it had supplemental training on anonymized Cursor workflow data. The fast variant is available only in Cursor and Grok Build. Release notes list only a US regional endpoint. **[V]** ([x.ai](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7), [release notes](https://docs.x.ai/developers/release-notes))

- **Muse Wonder (Oct 12).** Meta's first frontier model, via API at $4/$20 under the ID `muse-wonder-1.0`. The API is compatible with OpenAI and Anthropic SDKs. Wonder Full is available only through the Muse Code "Power Usage" and new "Maximum Usage" plans (Maximum = 80x the Everyday limit); plan prices not stated on the dev post ($50 / $200 figures: unverified). AA scores: Wonder Full 56, Wonder 52. Vendor-reported DeepSWE: 79.0 (Full) and 78.8. **[V]** ([Meta dev blog](https://dev.meta.ai/resources/blog/build-with-muse-wonder)); **[3P]** ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/metas-new-muse-wonder-and-muse-wonder-full-models-achieve-frontier-performance-but-only-the-weaker-one-is-available-via-api))

Self-hosting needs about 700GB of VRAM for NVFP4, or two M5 Ultra Mac Studios with MLX. Hosted on Groq, Fireworks, Together, Modal, Baseten and OpenRouter. **[V]** ([Meta](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-spark-open), [HF card](https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Spark-Open-816B-A42B))

Qwen 4 is reportedly coming "next week" (an unconfirmed slide shown in Amsterdam on Oct 29). **[V]** ([Alibaba](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/document-2021044032125272064), [vLLM](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-08-12-qwen3.8)); **[3P]** ([llms.blog](https://www.llms.blog/posts/qwen3-8-explained-max-the-open-2-4t-weights-flash-omni-flash-and-what-comes-with-qwen-4))

Vendor-reported: Terminal-Bench 4.0 41%. **[V]** ([Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/)); **[3P]** ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/mistral-large-4-le-chonk-arrives-as-the-top-open-model-outside-china-with-improved-coding-performance-and-63-lower-cost-per-task))

## 5. Reception (anecdotal unless stated)

- **GPT-6 Astra.** The HN launch thread (278 points) covered the botched rollout: the blog post 404'd and access was gated. In "Ask HN: Initial thoughts" (a small thread, 8 points), users called it "too-aligned," "very expensive. After 15 message I burned through my 5 hour limits," and said it "stops a lot." One poster benchmarked it about 30% faster than Sol at similar cost per outcome. **[3P]** ([HN 49554273](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49554273), [HN 49571621](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49571621), [HN 49572875](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49572875))
- **Opus 5.5.** The press framed it as frontier quality at a lower price (VentureBeat, Reuters). TechTarget cites CodeRabbit seeing output tokens up 50% on the same inputs. GitHub issues report weekly quota "draining between 30-50% per day" (#101937) and a jump in corrected answers since Oct 19 (#105112). ## 7. Hackathon implications

**What to build ("Real-Time AI" plus Durable Actors / Agent Relay):**
1. **Voice front end, durable agent back end.** GPT-Live-1's built-in delegation ("client delegation") is designed to keep the conversation going while a backend does the work. Make the backend a Terse **Durable Actor** per caller or session, which holds state and survives disconnects. Fan the work out to specialist agents over **Agent Relay**. This uses both hosts' products and the theme, and it maps directly to OpenAI's documented architecture. **[Inference]**
2. **Halloween demo idea: a multiplayer "haunted call line."** Several callers each have their own GPT-Live session, all talking to a shared haunted-house actor whose state persists. GPT-Live voices created with prompt-to-voice (free to create, Oct 16) give ghost characters distinct voices. Decisions running on Luna can trigger effects while the user is still speaking, which is a fast and visibly real-time trick. **[Inference]**
3. Gemini 3.8 Live is the alternative or fallback for **vision plus voice**: it accepts live video frames, its function calls don't block the conversation, and it has a free tier. GPT-Live-1 has no image input.

**Model picks for a 5-hour build on $100 of OpenAI credits:**
- Use **GPT-6.1 Sol ($2/$10)** or **Terra ($1/$5, cached $0.05)** as the reasoning backend, and **Luna ($0.10/$0.50, cached $0.01)** for routing and classification. OpenAI's own GPT-Live tool-use evals were run with a **Terra (low)** backend, which makes it the documented low-latency pairing. Avoid Astra ($10/$50, and HN users report it burns quota fast) except for one showcase call.
- GPT-Live-1 costs $0.05/min, so $100 buys about 2,000 voice minutes before backend costs. Voice minutes will not be the limiting cost.
- Keep prompts under 272K tokens. Above that, OpenAI charges 2x on input and 1.5x on output for the whole request.

**Gotchas:**
- GPT-Live uses a **new endpoint** (`v1/live/sessions`) with its own event schema (`session.start` → `session.started`). It is not `v1/realtime`, so older Realtime sample code won't work. Rate limits are counted in concurrent sessions, and **the Free tier is unsupported**, so check the account tier the credits land on.
- Astra, Sol and Terra don't support the Realtime or Live endpoints themselves. Only GPT-Live and gpt-realtime-* models handle audio.
- If you use Claude: Opus 5.5 does not allow disabling thinking or forcing tool use, and Sonnet 5.5 cyber refusals stop the request unless you opt in to fallbacks. Avoid security-themed demos (for example "hack the haunted mansion") on Claude 5.5 and Gemini 4, because of refusal risk.
- Gemini 4 Argon is not on the free tier, and Carbon is unavailable.
- Open weights (Muse Spark Open, Large 4, V4.1-Flash) are too large to self-host at the venue. Use them through Groq, Together or OpenRouter if at all.

**Setup before Oct 31:**
- **Check your OpenAI org's usage tier now.** GPT-Live-1 does not support the Free tier, and tier upgrades key off *purchased* credits. If your org shows Free, buy $5 of credits to reach Build (50 concurrent Live sessions) before Saturday rather than counting on the event's $100 grant to unlock it.
- Other credit sources that went live this quarter: eligible ChatGPT Pro/Business subscribers can claim up to $500/month in API credits (OpenAI changelog, Oct 28), and Anthropic added a monthly API credit for Claude Max and Team subscribers (Haiku 5.5 post, Oct 7). Amounts and eligibility unverified beyond those announcements.
- Run a GPT-Live WebRTC "hello world" on your own key, plus one Responses delegation call. Pre-create 2-3 prompt-to-voice ghost voices.
- Get a Gemini API key with a Live API test as a fallback. The Live models are free on the Gemini free tier (paid tier: $0.005/min in, $0.018/min out), so a free key works for the fallback path. Argon is paid-only.
- Pin model IDs in config (`gpt-6.1-sol`, `gpt-6.1-terra`, `gpt-6-luna`, `gpt-live-1`) so you can switch with one line.

**What judges and sponsors likely want [Inference]:** OpenAI is a sponsor, so a visible, well-built use of GPT-Live plus Agents or Responses delegation is on-message. Judges prioritize Durable Actors and Agent Relay, so the model is supporting cast, not the product. For Supabase's "scale to millions" prize, the Luna/Terra cost story (cents per thousand calls) gives a credible scaling claim to put on screen.
